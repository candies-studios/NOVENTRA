01 About Us

Built on Experience.Driven by Possibility.

Noventra Ventures is a new-age venture founded by two college friends with a shared ambition to build businesses around expertise, opportunity and long-term value creation.

Our journey combines entrepreneurial experience with a strong understanding of clients, markets and execution.

Today, Noventra operates across two focused business verticals — Marketing and MEP Consulting for Real Estate Projects.

We are intentionally focused, relationship-driven and committed to building businesses that stand the test of time.

02 Our Story

From a Shared Visionto a Shared Venture.

Noventra began with a simple idea: bring together complementary experience and build something meaningful.

The founders' journey across entrepreneurship, real estate contracts & marketing, client management and business development created the foundation for Noventra.

The company now brings this experience into specialised business verticals, working with organisations and projects that value strategic thinking, professional expertise and dependable execution.
